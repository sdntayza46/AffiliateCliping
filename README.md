# Dantez Cliping

Turn long stream replays and screen recordings into short 9:16 clips for affiliate & TikTok without touching a desktop editor. Fast, local, and zero nonsense.

## What It Does
- **Auto Highlights:** Finds the key moments from your recordings without manual scrubbing.
- **Multi-Clip Generation:** Spit out 1 to 5 unique clips per recording with varied cuts.
- **Framing & Crop:** 9:16 vertical view, status-bar crop, zoom & offset adjustments with live preview.
- **Anti-Reused Pacing:** Micro-speed and subtle noise to keep TikTok duplicate detection quiet.
- **In-App Updates:** Get new builds automatically inside the app.
- **Clean Dark UI:** High-contrast slate theme with no annoying glow or cluttered menus.
